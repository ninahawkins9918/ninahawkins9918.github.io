Playing

How is a grid read?

Each strip of five cells is one equation: a number, a sign, a number, an equals sign, a result. Strips run left to right or top to bottom, and a number at a crossing belongs to both of them. A grid is solved when every empty cell holds a number and every equation is true.

How do I put a number in?

Tap an empty cell, then a number in the tray at the bottom. Picking the number first and the cell second also works. Tapping a number you placed sends it back to the tray. Undo steps back one move at a time, all the way to the start.

What do the marks on an equation mean?

Once all three numbers of an equation are in, its equals sign changes: a filled circle when the equation holds, a crossed sign in a dashed ring when it does not. Equations with an empty cell show nothing. The marks can be turned off in Settings with Highlight equations.

Are there remainders, zeros or negative numbers?

No. Every number is a whole number from 1 up to 20 (Easy), 50 (Medium) or 99 (Hard). Division only appears when it comes out exact.

What is the difference between Open and One solution?

The switch on Home sets the mode for new grids. In One solution, each grid has exactly one correct fill. In Open, a grid may have several, and any fill that makes every equation true is a win, even if it differs from the one the app had in mind.

Size and difficulty seem unrelated. Is that intended?

Yes. Size (5×5, 7×7, 9×9) decides how many equations there are; difficulty decides the signs (Easy plus and minus, Medium adds times, Hard adds division), how large the numbers get and how many cells start empty. Each cell of the Home matrix starts one combination.

Hint, Undo and Start over

What does Hint do?

It places one number that keeps the grid solvable. If a number you placed has made the grid impossible, Hint places nothing and marks the cells to take back instead. Now and then, on a crowded grid, it asks you to remove a few numbers and ask again. Every press is counted and shown when the grid is solved; a best time is stored only for grids solved without Hint. A hinted number can be undone like any other.

What does Start over do?

In the grid's menu (the circle with three dots), Start over returns every placed number to the tray, clears the undo history and sets the clock to zero. Hints already used stay counted.

Today's grid and the streak

When does Today's grid change?

At midnight by your iPhone's date and time zone. Its size and difficulty follow the weekday: Monday 5×5 Easy, Tuesday 5×5 Medium, Wednesday 7×7 Easy, Thursday 5×5 Hard, Friday 7×7 Medium, Saturday 9×9 Easy, Sunday 7×7 Hard. It is always a One solution grid.

I finished after midnight. Which day counts?

The day on which you started that grid.

How does the streak work?

It counts consecutive days on which Today's grid was solved. A day without a solve ends it. Hints do not affect the streak. Flying across time zones can skip a calendar day or repeat one; the streak follows the dates your iPhone shows.

Does everyone get the same Today's grid?

The grid is made on your iPhone from the date. Players with the same date and the same app version normally see the same grid; different versions may produce different ones.

Progress and settings

Where are my results?

On Home, each cell of the matrix shows how many grids of that size and difficulty you have solved and your best time. Open and One solution keep separate results: flip the switch to see the other mode.

Can I reset my results?

There is no reset inside the app. Deleting Sumwright removes all results and saved grids.

Will my progress move to a new iPhone?

Only together with the rest of your iPhone, through an iCloud or computer backup or a direct transfer when the new phone is set up. Sumwright has no account and no sync of its own.

Settings in the app

The gear on Home opens Settings: Appearance (System, Light, Dark), Haptics, Show timer, Highlight equations and Show the intro again. The timer never limits play; hiding it only hides it.

Language

Sumwright speaks English, German, Italian and Dutch and follows the language of your iPhone. A different language for Sumwright alone can be picked in the iOS Settings app: open Sumwright's entry (listed under Apps on recent iOS versions) and choose Language.

Accessibility

VoiceOver reads every equation as a sentence ("blank plus 5 equals 12") and every cell by row and column. Marks on equations differ in shape, not only in color. At the largest text sizes the Home matrix becomes a list and the selected equation is shown in large digits above the grid.
